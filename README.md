# Free Interview Flashcards for Anki

Catalog of **free interview flashcard decks** for Anki, grouped by topic. Each deck has **100 cards** with structured questions and answers for technical interviews.

Published by [Tom Smykowski](https://tomasz-smykowski.com) · [Summon The JSON](https://summonthejson.com)

## How to use this catalog

1. Pick a topic below.
2. Open the deck repository.
3. Download the `.apkg` from **Releases** (no GitHub account required).
4. Import into [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) on your phone, tablet, or computer.

## Why these decks

- **Active recall:** practice retrieving answers, not just reading explanations.
- **Spaced repetition:** Anki reschedules cards you miss.
- **Interview depth:** structured answers, pitfalls, and follow-up angles.
- **Phone-friendly:** download and study on mobile without a laptop.
